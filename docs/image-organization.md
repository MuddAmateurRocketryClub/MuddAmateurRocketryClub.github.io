# Image organization

Images are named for their visible subjects, rather than the page where they appear. All legacy images were visually reviewed before renaming.

## Folders

- `branding/`: MARC logo.
- `club-life/`: campus, group, and workshop photos.
- `events/`: event-specific galleries.
- `hpr/`: individual rocket launches and HPR group photos.
- `propulsion/`, `recovionics/`, `structures/`: subteam work and hardware.
- `rockets/`: photos organized by rocket project.
- `space/`: general space imagery, currently unused by the site.
